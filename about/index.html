About

ASL for Teachers helps preservice teachers learn everyday classroom signs and the Deaf culture behind them, so they can build classrooms that welcome Deaf and hard of hearing students.

Created at Teachers College, Ball State University. All illustrations, hand drawings, sign cards, fonts, and lesson content are stored in this project, and the site does not load anything from outside services. [Add project team names.]

Accessibility Statement

We aim for this site to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Every page can be used with a keyboard, pictures have text descriptions, animations stop when your device is set to reduce motion, and text meets color contrast guidelines.

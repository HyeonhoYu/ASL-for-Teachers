## Scenarios

- **Morning Meeting:** GOOD MORNING, HELLO, NAME, HERE, HOW ARE YOU?, HAPPY, TIRED, FINE
- **Starting Group Work:** LOOK AT ME, PAY ATTENTION, START, WRITE, QUESTION, HELP, FINISH
- **Lining Up:** STAND, CLEAN UP, LINE UP, WAIT, SLOW
